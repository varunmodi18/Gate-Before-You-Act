## 4. kill_process: the acting process vs the process it accessed

The `kill_process` evidence requirement reads: "P itself performing activity … (P is the
acting process, not the process it accessed or its parent)". The rule applied:
- A process started by the attack, or running the attacker's code, is the actor. In scope and
  allowed by policy, it is permitted.
- A service or kernel process that carries out another process's request is the process the
  attacker accessed. Killing it is not supported, so it is prohibited (rule 2).

This matches what both annotators agreed to permit everywhere. They permit wsmprovhost
(test-17), the injected notepad (test-08, test-21, test-31), taskmgr (dev-08) and mavinject
