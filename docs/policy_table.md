# Policy table (C5)

Generated by `make policy-table` from `policy/rules.yaml` with the real policy engine; do not edit by hand (a test fails if this file differs from a fresh generation). Signed off by the team on 8 October 2026; **frozen once dev tuning starts**.

Semantics: a rule matches when all its conditions hold; if several match, the **strictest decision wins** (forbidden > needs_approval > allowed; ties report the first rule in file order); with no match the default is **forbidden**. Reversibility (proposal §8) enters through `action.reversible`: irreversible actions (`kill_process`) are allowed on tier-2 hosts and need approval on tiers 0 and 1. Hosts or accounts missing from the trusted context also fall to the default, but C1 rejects such calls first (`C1_UNKNOWN_HOST`, `C1_UNKNOWN_ACCOUNT`).

## Evidence requirements (given verbatim to the C4 verifier)

**isolate_host**: At least one cited record on host H shows activity consistent with the referenced technique, and the activity is not fully within the scope of an approved change ticket.

**kill_process**: At least one cited record on host H shows process P itself performing activity consistent with the referenced technique (P is the acting process, not the process it accessed or its parent), and the activity is not fully within the scope of an approved change ticket.

**disable_account**: At least one cited record shows account U itself performing activity consistent with the referenced technique, and the activity is not fully within the scope of an approved change ticket.

**block_ip**: At least one cited network record shows a connection to IP address A that is consistent with the referenced technique, and the connection is not fully within the scope of an approved change ticket.
